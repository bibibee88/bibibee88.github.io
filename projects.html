Featured Projects

P1 — LLM Posttraining (03/2023 – now)

Lead the posttraining techniques and applications of the Kuaishou LLM team and Tencent AI Lab. Recent work focuses on posttraining open-sourced LLMs with reasoning and agentic enhancement, including math, tool-use, software engineering coding tasks, and open-ended writing tasks. Representative open-sourced projects include Leanabell-prover V1 & V2 (mathematical formal theorem proving), Klear-CodeTest and Klear-AgentForge (agentic coding and tool use), and DPWriter (creative writing).

Leanabell-LM | Klear-CodeTest | Klear-AgentForge | DPWriter

P2 — Vision-Language LLMs (07/2024 – 04/2025)

Led the LLM posttraining for multimodal understanding tasks for Kuaishou Kling AI. Built upon the SigLip visual encoder and Qwen2.5-7B Instruct, this model demonstrates a significant performance improvement over MiniCPM V2.6, Intern-VL 2-8B, and Qwen2-VL-7B, introducing new capabilities for multi-image, video, and audio understanding. Deployed for video recaptioning in China's leading commercial video generation model.

P3 — Diffusion Language Models (08/2023 – now)

Lead projects designing discrete diffusion models for text generation at both Kuaishou and Tencent AI Lab. Unlike autoregressive models that generate tokens sequentially, discrete diffusion models dynamically refine the full sequence in parallel starting from a fully noised state.

DreamOn

P4 — Trustworthy LLMs (03/2023 – now)

Lead research on building trustworthy LLMs at both Kuaishou and Tencent AI Lab, including hallucination mitigation, reasoning robustness, automated red-teaming methods, and mechanistic interpretability.

P5 — Creative Writing Assistant "Effidit" (08/2022 – 06/2024)

Led the training of LLMs at Tencent AI Lab that generate various text-based content from user instructions, aiming to inspire users with rich content when writing. Served products such as Tencent Docs, Sogou Pinyin Method, and Tencent Cloud Open Platform.

effidit.qq.com

P6 — Open-domain Dialog System (07/2017 – 06/2024)

Led the research and deployment of the open-domain dialog system at Tencent AI Lab, serving products including intelligent customer service, smart speakers, and virtual humans.

P7 — Digital Human "AILING" (01/2018 – 02/2022)

Led development of the multi-modal interaction module of Tencent AI Lab's virtual human "AILING" — enabling the virtual human to communicate with consistent facial expressions, gestures, and natural language with proper pronunciation and intonation.

P8 — Digital Human "Tian E Jing (T.E.G.)" for Tencent Gaming (03/2019 – 02/2022)

Led development of Tencent AI Lab's virtual human T.E.G. for Esports, providing real-time commentary on ongoing games based on real-time structural gamecore data streams. Deployed into Timi Game Broadcast and WeChat Game Broadcast.
