BIO

I am joining the School of Artificial Intelligence, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) as an Associate Professor. I previously worked as a Principal Researcher and Lead of the LLM Posttraining team at Kuaishou, and spent seven years as a Principal Researcher at Tencent AI Lab, with earlier experience at Baidu NLP and as a Visiting Scholar at the National University of Singapore. My research focuses on large language models and vision-language models — particularly posttraining techniques that enhance reasoning, agentic abilities, and cross-modal understanding — along with natural language processing and machine learning more broadly. I have led industrial research teams that shipped widely-used systems, including open-domain dialog systems, multi-modal digital humans, and creative writing assistants, and have released open-source projects spanning formal theorem proving, agentic coding, and diffusion language models. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and my B.S. in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University.
