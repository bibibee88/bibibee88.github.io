Bio

I am a tenure-track Associate Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). I received my bachelor's degree from Sun Yat-sen University in 2010 and my PhD from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2015. Before returning to academia in 2026, I spent most of my post-PhD career in industrial research, including seven years at Tencent.

My current research focuses on large language models and vision-language models, with three main directions: (i) emergent capabilities, including reasoning, agents, and AI-driven scientific discovery; (ii) model trustworthiness and interpretability; and (iii) foundations of next-generation language models, including diffusion language models and neural architecture design.
